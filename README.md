# CalcHub

Free online calculators in 7 languages (English, Slovak, German, Czech, Polish, French, Spanish).

**Website: [calc-hub.eu](https://calc-hub.eu)**

## What's inside
- **Health:** BMI, calorie needs, water intake, body fat, ideal weight
- **Finance:** mortgage, loan payment, VAT, compound interest, currency and Bitcoin converter
- **Construction:** paint coverage, concrete volume, tiles, roof pitch, wall area
- **Energy and car:** electricity cost, fuel consumption, solar panels, EV charging
- **Utilities:** unit converter, percentage, date difference, time zones

Every calculator includes a short explanation of the formula and works in any browser. No sign-up, no data stored on your device.

## Tech
Static HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step.
